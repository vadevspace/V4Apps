More about your potential benefits

Social Security paid by VA6.2% of taxable wages on behalf of the employee. If taxable wages exceed the taxable maximum of $176,100, VA contributes $10,918.
Medicare paid by VA1.45% on behalf of the employee.
Life InsuranceVA pays 1/3 of the cost of basic life insurance.
Thrift Savings Plan (TSP) Federal 401(k)VA provides up to 5% employer contributions (1% automatic contribution and up to 4% employer matching contributions).
Federal Employee Retirement System (FERS)For employees hired after 1/1/2014, VA contributes 16.5% annually to the FERS-FRAE basic/defined benefit traditional pension.
VHA National Child Care ProgramAfter 60 days of employment, full-time employees with a total family income below $149,000 may be eligible for the childcare subsidy program (conditions apply).
Flexible Spending Account (FSA)Contribute up to $3,200 a year to a health care FSA with a $640 maximum carry-over, and save up to 30% on dependent care expenses.
Employee Assistance Program (EAP)Free legal and medical services and up to 8 hours of authorized absence per year.
Transit Benefit ProgramYou may qualify for a transit subsidy of up to $265/month; check with your local VA facility (conditions apply).
Student Loan Repayment Program (SLRP)For hard-to-recruit and retain positions, you may receive up to $40,000 per year and up to $100,000 total toward student loans in return for a 3-year service agreement.
Competitive Salaries & Regular IncreasesPay is based on grade, step and duty location. Receive annual increases when signed into law, plus within-grade “step” increases every year for 3 years and then every 2–3 years.
Employment Without StringsFull- or part-time, VA employment comes without employment contracts, noncompete clauses, restrictive covenants or other significant restrictions on outside work.

*This calculation is a sample only and does not constitute a contract of employment or a guarantee of benefits or future employment. Certain elements vary and are based on specific circumstances. Paid leave is not an additional payment but is the value of your paid time off included in your annual salary based on an 8-hour day. If your service includes part-time calculations, your total rewards and benefits will be proportionally prorated. For more information, please see www.opm.gov.
